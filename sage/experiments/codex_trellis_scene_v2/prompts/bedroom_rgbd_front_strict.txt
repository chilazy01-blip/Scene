Create one clean, well-organized modern bedroom for an indoor RGB-D reconstruction dataset.

Mandatory object instances (every line is required; these are not examples):
- exactly one bed;
- exactly two bedside tables, one on each side of the bed;
- exactly one desk and exactly one office chair positioned as a usable workstation;
- exactly one wardrobe;
- exactly two table lamps, one centered on each bedside table;
- exactly one stack of books and exactly one cup on the desk;
- exactly two storage boxes placed neatly under the desk or beside the wardrobe;
- exactly two pillows on the bed;
- exactly two separate slippers forming one left/right pair beside the bed.

Spatial requirements:
- keep the bed and its two bedside tables as a centered, approximately symmetric primary group;
- preserve a clear route from the entry to the bed, desk, and wardrobe;
- show every required object with complete visible shapes, clear boundaries, minimal occlusion,
  and an explicit functional relationship to nearby objects;
- keep surfaces organized and avoid adding decorative clutter beyond the mandatory list.

Camera requirements (strict): use a strict front-facing view from the south side looking north,
use a centered composition with the camera on the room centerline, keep an eye-level camera at
about 1.55 m, and use reduced perspective distortion with a moderate telephoto focal length.

Rendering requirements: uniform bright diffuse lighting, stable exposure, no dramatic shadows,
realistic moderate textures, clean geometry, and an uncluttered RGB-D dataset appearance that is
friendly to reconstruction, depth estimation, and semantic segmentation.
